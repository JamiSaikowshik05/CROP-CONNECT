About CropConnect

The CropConnect is an official Government of India initiative to facilitate transparent and efficient procurement of paddy and groundnut from farmers at Minimum Support Price (MSP).

Key Features
  • Online farmer registration with Aadhaar verification
  • Slot booking system for procurement
  • Transparent MSP-based payment calculation
  • Digital invoice generation
  • Real-time status tracking
